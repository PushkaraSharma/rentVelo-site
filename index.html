What does RentVelo include?

Why choose RentVelo?

Purpose-built for Indian landlords. Powerful enough for property managers. Simple enough for everyone.

Property & Tenant Management

Add and organize all your rental units — houses, flats, PGs, buildings. Track tenant details and occupancy at a glance.

PDF rent receipts

Generate professional, minimalist rent receipts instantly. Share via WhatsApp or email in one tap. No more handwritten slips.

Electricity reading tracker

Integrated meter reading tracker with continuity checks. Auto-calculates electricity charges per unit every month.

Expense & balance tracking

Log expenses, track outstanding balances, and see your complete financial picture across all properties in one place.

Google Drive backup

Your data, secured in your own Google Drive. Switch phones without losing anything. Full restore in seconds.

Smart payment reminders

Automated local push notifications for due dates. Deep-linked — tap the notification and land directly on that tenant's record.

Automated penalty accrual

Set grace periods, configure daily late fees, and let RentVelo calculate penalties automatically. No more manual math or awkward conversations.
