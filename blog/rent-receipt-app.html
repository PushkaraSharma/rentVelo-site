Why tenants still ask for a slip

A UPI screenshot is not a receipt. Tenants need a dated slip for office reimbursement, income-tax house-rent claims, or simply to prove the month is closed. Landlords still write that slip by hand, take a photo, and send it. The photo is blurry. The amount is wrong. The next month, nobody can find it.

A rent receipt app prints the same record the landlord already marked as paid: tenant name, property, month, amount, and date. RentVelo turns that into a PDF you share from the phone.

What a useful rent receipt includes

A receipt that survives an office or a tax query has more than “₹12,000 received”:

  • Who paid — tenant name attached to that room, not a nickname from WhatsApp.
  • Which month — so a late January payment is not confused with February rent.
  • How much — rent plus extras if you billed electricity on the same month.
  • When it was issued — a date the tenant can file.

Handwritten pads skip one of those every time someone is in a hurry. The PDF from RentVelo uses the payment you already logged in the rent management app.

Share on WhatsApp, keep the record

Indian rent conversations live on WhatsApp. The receipt has to leave the app the same way. RentVelo generates the PDF on the device and you send it. You do not upload tenant data to a receipt website, and you do not email yourself a scan later.

The month stays marked paid in the collection list. If the tenant asks again in March, you resend the same January receipt instead of rewriting it.

Receipts without a desktop printer

Some property software assumes you print on letterhead from a laptop. Plenty of landlords collect rent from a two-wheeler or after work. If the receipt needs a printer, the pad wins. A phone-first rent receipt app matches how the money already moves — UPI, then a file on WhatsApp.

When a receipt app is not enough

If you need digitally signed Form 16A or a CA-stamped rent certificate, use an accountant. RentVelo covers the everyday landlord receipt: proof of the month, shared in a chat, stored with the tenant record. Pair it with meter readings on the electricity rent tracker when the bill is not a flat amount.
