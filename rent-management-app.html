What landlords actually search for

Most owners are not looking for a desktop property ERP. They search for a rent management app or a rent collection app because the work happens on a phone: a tenant says they paid, a receipt is missing, someone is two days late, and you are not at a laptop.

RentVelo is built for that moment. Add properties and tenants, mark rent as paid or pending, share a PDF receipt, and see expected versus collected — on iOS and Android.

Spreadsheets still lose months

A sheet can store a name and an amount. It cannot tell you, on the 7th, who is still pending across three PGs. It does not generate a receipt. Late fees get calculated in a chat. When a tenant switches rooms, payment history lives in the wrong row.

Rent management is not “add a line every month.” Occupancy changes. Partial payments happen. Electricity readings pile up. If the record is not attached to the tenant, you argue about what was already paid.

What a rent management app should do on a phone

A useful landlord app does the jobs you already do by hand:

  • Track collection — expected, collected, and pending rent on one dashboard.
  • Keep properties and tenants together — rooms, occupancy, and the people who live there.
  • Mark monthly rent as paid, partial, or pending without hunting through chats.
  • Share a PDF rent receipt on WhatsApp or email instead of a handwritten slip.
  • Remind yourself with local due-date notifications, not another spreadsheet formula.

RentVelo also tracks balances and optional electricity readings. Data stays on your device, with backup to your own Google Drive when you want it. That is rent collection software Indian landlords can actually use — rupees, UPI links, WhatsApp receipts — without giving a SaaS company the tenant file.

UPI without a middleman

Indian rent is often paid on UPI. RentVelo does not collect or settle that money. A pay link opens the tenant’s UPI app. You mark the month when the payment lands. No commission, no waiting on a third-party ledger.

Why phone-first beats desktop-only software

Many property tools assume you sit at a browser. Plenty of landlords manage one building or a few flats from the road. The phone is already in the pocket. If basic receipts and pending rent need a desktop, the spreadsheet wins again.

RentVelo is phone-first, with light and dark themes. You do not need a second screen to know who to follow up with today.

Is RentVelo the rent management app for every landlord?

If you need biometric gates, multi-branch accounting, or a tenant marketplace, look at heavier property software. If you need a rent management app to replace the sheet — collection status, receipts, balances, local data — RentVelo is built for that job and is live on the App Store and Google Play.
