Who the rent management app is for

A rent management app here means the tool an Indian landlord uses to close the month: who paid, the receipt, and the pending list. The India-only wording of that search is the same job, not a second product.

The choices people already compare are a spreadsheet, RentOk, Rentrovio, Zoho’s property app, and a phone record that never sits in the payment.

What the phone record still has to do

RentVelo is the last row. A pay link opens the tenant’s UPI app. You mark the month when the money lands. The file stays on the phone, with optional Google Drive backup.

  • Collection — expected, collected, and pending on one list.
  • Receipt — a PDF rent receipt you can resend on WhatsApp.
  • Electricity — an optional reading on the same month, in the electricity tracker.

When this is the wrong rent management app

Pick RentOk or Rentrovio if you want tenants to pay inside their product. Pick Zoho’s property app if you want a browser ledger. Pick a spreadsheet if you have one tenant who always pays on the 1st. Pick heavier property software if you need biometric gates, a tenant marketplace, or multi-branch accounting.

Pick RentVelo if the remaining job is owner-side: properties, tenants, paid or pending, and a receipt, without a company in the middle of the UPI. It is on the App Store and Google Play.
