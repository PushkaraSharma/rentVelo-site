A PG is not one flat

A paying guest house is a stack of rooms. People move mid-month. Two tenants share a meter. Someone on the second floor paid cash and someone on the first sent UPI without a note. The “best PG management app” is the one that keeps the room, the person, and the month attached when you are standing in the corridor.

A sheet can list 12 names. It cannot tell you, on the 7th, which bed is empty and which electricity reading belongs to last month’s tenant who already left.

What a PG app has to do on a phone

  • Rooms and occupancy — who lives where, not a nickname in a WhatsApp group.
  • Monthly rent per tenant — paid, partial, pending across the building.
  • Shared or split electricity — a reading that sits on the same month as rent. See the electricity tracker.
  • A receipt you can resend — when a parent asks for March in June. The receipt guide covers the slip.

If the tool needs a desktop to change a room, the register on the wall wins again.

When you should not pick RentVelo

If you run a large hostel with biometric entry, meal plans, and a night manager, look at hostel software. If you only have one tenant in one flat, a sheet or the free rent apps comparison is enough. RentVelo is the middle: a PG or a few buildings, collected by the owner, on iOS and Android.

How we use “best” here

Best for a 16-room PG in a tier-2 city is not best for a branded coliving chain. We are not ranking every product in India. We are saying: if the pain is occupancy plus late rent plus a shared meter, a phone-first rent management app is the category that fits — and RentVelo is the one we ship for that job.
