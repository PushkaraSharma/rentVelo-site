RentVelo is our rent management app. This page compares jobs — spreadsheet, cloud SaaS, phone-first — not invented star ratings or someone else’s price list.

What people type into Google

The searches look different: best rent app, best rent property app, best property rent management apps free, best rent app in India. The job is the same. A landlord wants collection status, a receipt for WhatsApp, and a balance that matches what the tenant paid — without a desktop and without a commission on every UPI.

A useful list does not crown one winner for every building. It tells you which tool fits how you already collect rent.

What “free” actually means

Free trial is not free collection. Many cloud products let you add two flats, then charge per unit, per receipt, or a cut of the rent they process. The app looks free in the Play Store. The month is not free when you settle.

A free rent management app in the landlord sense should let you mark paid or pending, share a PDF, and keep the file on your phone — without taking a slice of the UPI. RentVelo does not collect or settle rent. The tenant pays you. You mark the month. That is the free that matters in India.

When the spreadsheet is still the best rent app

If you have one apartment and one tenant who always pays on the 1st, a sheet is enough. You do not need a rent management app to store a name and an amount. The sheet loses when a second PG comes online, someone pays partial, or you need last March’s receipt in a chat.

When cloud property software is worth paying for

Pay for SaaS if you need a tenant login, a public listing, or an accountant who lives in a browser. Those products are a different job from “mark this room paid and send a slip.” If you are leaving a product like that because of fees, read our RentOK alternatives note.

Where RentVelo fits as a free rent property app

RentVelo is the phone-first option on this list. Add properties and tenants, see expected versus collected, share a PDF rent receipt, and keep electricity on the same month if you need it. It is not a tenant marketplace and not a 200-unit ERP. If you run a PG, the PG management write-up is more specific.
